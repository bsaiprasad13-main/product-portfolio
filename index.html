// 01 — About

Engineering physics, product instincts

I'm a final-year B.Tech in Engineering Physics at IIT Madras (Class of 2027), currently going through the NextLeap PM Fellowship. I got here the unconventional way — I deliberately extended my graduation timeline to build product skills instead of following the standard engineering-to-core-job path.

That instinct came from three years running large-scale operations at Saarang, IIT Madras's flagship cultural festival, where I coordinated cross-functional teams and logistics for tens of thousands of attendees. Somewhere in that chaos, I realized I liked solving for people and systems more than I liked solving for physics problem sets.

Since then, my approach has been consistent: don't just write PRDs for hypothetical products — build the real thing, ship it, and let the usage data make the case. Everything in my project section below is live or was live, not a Figma mockup.
